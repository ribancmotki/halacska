Living Reef · Hall 04

Find her in the anemones.

The clownfish you're watching will spend her entire adult life — often twenty years — within reach of one host anemone. A protein-rich mucus coat lets her pass through tentacles that would paralyze any other reef fish. In return, she drives off the butterflyfish that would otherwise graze the brain coral and acropora bare.
